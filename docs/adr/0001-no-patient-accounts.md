# No Patient Accounts; DNI and One-time Links as Identity

Patients book, cancel, and reschedule without creating an account; the clinic recognizes them by their DNI, and the only authorization to manage an appointment online is a single-use one-time link sent by email. Chosen so the clinic runs no account system and patients book in under a minute; the trade-off is that email-less bookings (front desk/phone) cannot be managed online.
